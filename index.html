I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Colorado Boulder, advised by Rebecca Morrison. I work on transport and diffusion modeling and graph structure learning.

In what now feels like a previous life, I was a research analyst at The Economist Group, where I led the India forecasts on the economy, politics and policy. And prior to that I was formally trained in economics and international relations.
